# Editing an existing entry

Open any existing entry in a section Ghostwriter writes for, and click **Edit with Ghostwriter**.

The panel opens with the entry's content, as it stands, as the draft. There is no brief: the entry is the brief.

## Asking for changes

Ask for what you want in plain words:

- "Tighten the introduction."
- "Rewrite this for a client who has never commissioned a garden."
- "Add a short section on maintenance before the closing paragraph."

The draft on the right shows the result, and you can click any text to change it yourself, as when [writing](writing.md#the-draft).

## Use these changes

**Use these changes** puts the revised writing into your own Craft draft of the entry: the "edited, not saved" draft Craft keeps for each person. The form reloads to show it.

- **Only the writing changes.** Images, links, related entries, settings, and blocks that are switched off stay exactly as they were.
- **Blocks are matched by type, in order.** The entry's second text block takes the draft's second text block, even if the writer moved it. New blocks are added; blocks the writer removed are removed.
- **The live entry is untouched** until you save. To throw the changes away, discard your draft as usual in Craft.

## Coming back

If you close the panel, or reload the page, before using the changes, they are kept. **Edit with Ghostwriter** picks up the same conversation.

To start again from the entry as it is saved, and throw away the changes asked for, click **Start again from the entry**.

Once changes have gone into the entry, the next **Edit with Ghostwriter** starts from the entry again, so anything you've changed by hand is included.

## What is not changed

Editing never applies [house style](fields.md#house-style) or [image placeholders](images.md#placeholders). Those are for building new entries; an existing entry keeps its own settings, links and images.
